Welcome to Calostus

A place to slow down and savour life.

On the coast of Palaia Fokaia, Calostus is a gastrotavern where simplicity meets quality. The fish comes in every morning, the vegetables come from producers along the Saronic coast, and almost everything meets charcoal, lemon and Greek extra virgin olive oil — very little else.

It is the kind of place where a long lunch turns into an afternoon, and a drink before dinner turns into dinner. The tables face west, so nobody is in a hurry once the sun starts going down.
